Who We Are

About The HAS

Founded by the pioneering swimming clubs of Harare and built over decades by dedicated volunteers,coaches and parents.

OBJECTIVES

What We Are Expecting To Do

  • To identify, nurture and develop swimmers from learn to swim level to elite national and international level through structured training and competition.
VISION

OUR ASPIRATION

  • To consistently produce national champions and swimmers who represent Zimbabwe ata CANA Zone IV, Africa Aquatics Championships,African Games and Commonwealth Gamess
MISSION

OUR MANDATE

  • To recruit,train and certify coaches,technical officials,and team managers to national and international standards.
